About the Clinic

Physician-led, technology-forward skin care

Lonsdale Skin & Laser Clinic is North Vancouver's dedicated centre for advanced aesthetic procedures and laser treatments. We pair cutting-edge technology and expert knowledge with unbiased, physician-led medical advice — building custom-made treatment packages tailored to you following a complimentary consultation, with no commitment required.

Led by board-certified aesthetic physician Dr. Willem Gouws, our approach centres on open communication and informed decisions, so every treatment is chosen with honest guidance and your goals in mind. From laser hair removal and IPL photofacials to RF microneedling, injectables, thread lifts and non-surgical body contouring, we help you look and feel your best.
